Theme 01 · Lessons

How to know

See clearly. Test hard. Fail usefully. Prefer evidence over authority — and treat “I don’t know” as strength.

Lessons

Work through them in order

Each lesson is a Concept Development Study taught Socratically. Keep a notebook; answer every pause before you read on.

  1. 1

    Seeing vs believing

    Observation is not belief. Falsifiable claims take a risk; empty ones don’t. “I don’t know — here is how we’d find out” is where inquiry starts. Includes an Occam note on the road mirage.

    Start Lesson 1 →
  2. 2

    Falsifiability

    A claim is scientific only if something could prove it wrong. Spot claims rescued by excuses or hidden in vagueness, then design a test — fixed in advance — that could kill your own favourite idea. Includes an Occam note on the cost of excuses.
