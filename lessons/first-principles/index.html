First principles

Strip problems to what is true. Rebuild from axioms. Separate nature’s limits from culture’s habits — including jobs and money.

Lessons

Work through them in order

Each lesson is a Concept Development Study taught Socratically. Keep a notebook; answer every pause before you read on.

  1. 1

    Strip it to what is true

    Judge what is possible from what a thing is made of and what physics charges — not from habit or analogy. Build an energy floor for a kettle and for aluminium, test it against real figures, and read the gap above it as nature’s limit or ours. Includes an Occam note on the emperor’s cutlery and a kettle audit you can do at home.
