Start here: the battery in your drawer

Most homes have an old phone or laptop battery in a drawer. It no longer holds a charge, but it still contains every atom of metal it was made with. Why does it matter what happens to it next?

Pause and answer
  1. Which metals do you think are inside a lithium-ion battery?
  2. Why would someone pay to collect old batteries?
  3. Why is throwing one in the bin dangerous?
Compare your reasoning

Lithium, nickel, cobalt, manganese, copper and aluminium, plus graphite and plastic. Some of these, especially cobalt and nickel, are valuable enough that recyclers pay for cobalt-rich batteries. Lithium-ion batteries are classed as Class 9 hazardous materials for transport, so they need their own route, not the rubbish bin. (EverBatt)

Where the value is

Where is the value in a spent lithium-ion battery?

One kilowatt-hour of NMC622 cells, the kind used in many electric cars, weighs about 4.6 kg (EverBatt’s default cell: 0.168 kWh in 0.772 kg, Argonne EverBatt). A hydrometallurgical plant uses about 3.2 MJ of energy per kg of cells, so about 4.1 kWh for that 4.6 kg. Here is what the recovered metals are worth at October 2026 prices, ranked. Rupee conversions use Fed H.10.

Where this sits on the value quadrant: Form, at the end of time. Recycling changes a dead battery’s form back into materials, so the value of the metal is used a second time. See the quadrant.

Working: how each estimate was made
  • Mass per kWh. 0.772 kg ÷ 0.168 kWh ≈ 4.60 kg/kWh. Copper 0.161 × 4.60 ≈ 0.74 kg; aluminium 0.084 × 4.60 ≈ 0.39 kg.
  • Nickel. 0.61 × 0.98 × $16.324/kg × ₹94.94/$ ≈ ₹927. Copper. 0.74 × 0.90 × $14.474 × 94.94 ≈ ₹915.
  • Lithium. 0.13 × 0.90 × 5.3 ≈ 0.62 kg carbonate × 93.6 yuan/kg × ₹14.23/yuan ≈ ₹826, where ₹94.94 ÷ 6.6702 yuan per $ ≈ ₹14.23.
  • Cobalt. 0.19 × 0.98 × 200–235 yuan/kg × 14.23 ≈ ₹530–623.
  • Process energy. EverBatt’s hydrometallurgical route uses 0.6 MJ diesel + 2.5 MJ natural gas + 0.125 MJ electricity = 3.225 MJ per kg of cells; 3.225 × 4.60 ÷ 3.6 ≈ 4.1 kWh per kWh of battery. Value per kWh of process energy = value ÷ 4.1.
  • Caution. This is contained value at metal and carbonate prices, not a recycler’s revenue. It leaves out chemicals (per kg of cells: 1.08 kg sulfuric acid, 0.561 kg sodium hydroxide, 0.366 kg hydrogen peroxide, EverBatt), labour, collection, refining discounts and plant cost. The Faraday metal figures are per kWh of battery and the EverBatt figures per kWh of cells, so the mix is approximate. Cell mass per kWh varies by design (TBC for Indian packs).

Business words on this page (unit cost, gross margin, contribution margin and others) are defined in plain language in the glossary. Figures marked “our estimate” are our arithmetic from the cited prices; they are for learning, not business or investment advice.

What you can do with this

  • Small business: price the feed, not just the output

    EverBatt’s industry figures show recyclers paying about $2/kg for cobalt-rich LCO batteries and being paid about $2/kg to take LFP (EverBatt). Rows 1–4 explain why. Work out the contribution margin per kg for each chemistry before choosing what to collect, and register on the CPCB Battery EPR portal first.

  • Skill: learn to tell chemistries apart

    Rows 1 and 4 (nickel and cobalt) carry most of the value in NMC; LFP has neither. Sorting by chemistry is a skill a collection point needs before any unit economics can work. Labels and the producer’s EPR registration are the starting point.

    Who hires for this and which fairs to watch: NEST Collective → Manufacturing floor

  • Home habit: return, don’t bin

    Under the Battery Waste Management Rules, producers must take back waste batteries and meet recovery targets (BWMR 2022). Ask the shop or brand for its take-back point. A phone battery in a drawer is metal nobody can use.

04

The dated target

India’s rules set how much material must be recovered from waste batteries.

Benchmark for this page: the Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 set minimum recovery of materials from waste portable and electric-vehicle batteries of 70% for 2024-25, 80% for 2025-26 and 90% from 2026-27, and for automotive and industrial batteries of 55%, 60% and 60% (BWMR 2022; see also the consolidated rules to 2025).

Worked: what 90% means for one kWh

Applied to the five metals in the value table, 90% of the contained ≈ ₹3,310–3,400 would be about ₹3,000. Note the rule counts recovered material, not rupees; this is only a way to picture it.

05

Try it: a battery audit at home

Find out how many lithium cells your home holds.

You need: 15 minutes and a box.

  1. Collect every dead or unused rechargeable battery in the house: phones, laptops, power banks, toys, tools.
  2. Read each label: chemistry (Li-ion, LiFePO₄, NiMH, lead-acid) and watt-hours if shown.
  3. Add up the watt-hours. Using this page’s ≈ ₹3,300 per kWh for NMC, what is the contained metal worth?
  4. Find the brand’s or shop’s take-back point and return them taped at the terminals.
06

Limits: why recycling is hard

Contained value is not profit.

  • Chemistry mix. LFP cells contain no nickel or cobalt, so recyclers are paid to take them (EverBatt).
  • Costs left out. Chemicals, labour, plant, collection and refining are not in the value table.
  • Safety. Lithium-ion cells are hazardous goods in transport; packs need discharge and dismantling before shredding.
  • Price swings. Lithium and cobalt prices move widely; this page’s figures are dated (30 Oct 2026 for lithium and cobalt, Sep 2026 for nickel, copper and aluminium) and will be out of date soon.
  • Data. India-specific recycler costs and energy use are TBC.
02 · Distance from normal

How far the records go from normal

Recycling: mostly close to normal

Normal is 288 K and one atmosphere. Leaching, extraction and precipitation are wet chemistry in tanks; EverBatt costs its leaching reactors as rated from atmospheric pressure to 25 psi (EverBatt). The hottest steps are calcination and, on the pyrometallurgical route, smelting; their temperatures are TBC for this page. The distance from normal is mostly chemical (strong acid and alkali) rather than thermal.

03 · Review

Check your reasoning

Answer each one in your notebook before you open it.

Which two metals carry most of the contained value in an NMC622 cell?

Nickel (≈ ₹927 per kWh) and copper (≈ ₹915), then lithium (≈ ₹826) and cobalt (≈ ₹530–623).

Why are recyclers paid to take LFP batteries but pay for LCO?

LFP has no nickel or cobalt; LCO is rich in cobalt, the most valuable metal per kg.

Which route uses the most energy per kg in EverBatt?

Pyrometallurgical, about 5.3 MJ per kg of cells, against about 3.2 for hydrometallurgical.

What recovery target do India’s rules set for EV batteries from 2026-27?

At least 90% of materials recovered.
