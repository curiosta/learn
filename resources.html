Refresher · ~20 min

TRIZ refresher

TRIZ, the theory of inventive problem solving, grew from Genrich Altshuller's study of patents in the USSR from 1946. This refresher covers technical and physical contradictions, the contradiction matrix and the 40 inventive principles, the Ideal Final Result, resources, separation principles, and trends of evolution — then works Lesson 1's 30-second kettle through them. The point to carry away: first principles find the floor set by physics; TRIZ helps close the gap above it, and cannot beat the floor.
