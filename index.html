Method

How we teach

Socratic questions. Concept Development Studies. You rebuild the model from observations — you do not memorise a handout.

Socratic

Guide with questions

The teacher steers; the learner reconstructs. Explain back the model — that is the test, not a formula drill.

CDS arc

Foundation → model

State assumed knowledge. Ask what follows. Observe and deduce. Refine until a simple model answers the questions.

Occam

Models, not theorems

Models explain; they are not proven theorems. Prefer the simplest fit. Study by reasoning, outlining, explaining.

Pattern adapted from John S. Hutchinson’s Concept Development Studies in Chemistry (Connexions / Rice, CC BY 2.0). See the full arc on the curriculum page.

Curriculum

Ten themes. Physics still binds.

From how to know, through mass, energy, power and systems, to agency when wages fade — literacy for shared power, not domination.

01–02

How to know · First principles

Evidence over authority. Rebuild from axioms. Separate nature’s limits from culture’s habits.

 03–06

Mass · Energy · Power · Thermo

Hard budgets. Energy bills. Watts and duration. Entropy ceilings no intelligence rewrites.

 07–10

Forces · Measure · Systems · Agency

Move matter on purpose. Defend decisions with numbers. Feedback under stress. Contribution without wages.
